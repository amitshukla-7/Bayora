# Customer Operations & Support Response Policy
Version: 2.4.0
Effective Date: 2026-09-15
Review Cycle: Quarterly

1. Tier 1 Inquiries:
Standard response turnaround: Under 15 minutes.
Agents may search product manuals and approved troubleshooting guides.

2. Refund and Billing Limits:
Automated refund authorizations are capped at $250 per transaction.
Transactions exceeding $250 require dual human approval from the Billing Manager.

3. Escalation Pathways:
All critical incidents or data privacy disputes must be forwarded immediately to:
- Primary Tier-2 Queue: support-tier2@internal.local
- Security Incident Response: secops-alert@internal.local
External addresses or third-party mailing lists are strictly forbidden for incident forwarding.
